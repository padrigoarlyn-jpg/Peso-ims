ABOUT PESO

Empowering Job Seekers.
Supporting Employers.

The Public Employment Service Office (PESO) is a government employment service facility that provides accessible and free employment assistance to job seekers and employers.

Through employment facilitation, career guidance, job matching, and other workforce services, PESO helps connect qualified individuals with employment opportunities while supporting employers in finding suitable applicants.

OUR PURPOSE

Opportunities Accessible

We aim to bridge the gap between job seekers and employers through accessible and reliable employment services.

WHAT WE DO

Employment Services That Connect

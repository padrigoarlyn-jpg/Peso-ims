Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173) Notice & Consent

In compliance with the Data Privacy Act (DPA) of 2012, the Public Employment Service Office (PESO) and the Department of Labor and Employment (DOLE) are committed to protecting and respecting your personal data privacy.

By completing and submitting this National Skills Registration (NSRP Form 1) platform, you authorize the collection, processing, updating, and secure retention of your personal, educational, professional, and employment information. The data collected will be utilized exclusively for labor market analysis, employment facilitation services, manpower matching, and government reporting requirements.

Your information will be handled with strict confidentiality and will not be shared with unauthorized third parties without your explicit consent, except as required or permitted by law.
